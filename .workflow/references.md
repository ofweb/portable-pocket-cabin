# External references

## Biomes O' Plenty

- Link: [Project repository](https://github.com/Glitchfiend/BiomesOPlenty).
- Relevance: Adds biomes, trees, plants, and flowers across dimensions. These can broaden generated exploration requirements.
- Related: [Upgrade materials and exploration](direction.md#upgrade-materials-and-exploration).
- Unverified: Compatible versions and suitable material profiles for this project's Minecraft version.

## Alex's Mobs

- Link: [Project repository](https://github.com/AlexModGuy/AlexsMobs), [loader declaration](https://github.com/AlexModGuy/AlexsMobs/blob/1.20/src/main/resources/META-INF/mods.toml).
- Relevance: Intended source of animal-related materials and encounters for cabin progression.
- Related: [Upgrade materials and exploration](direction.md#upgrade-materials-and-exploration).
- Unverified: Compatibility with this project's Fabric and Minecraft versions. The inspected default branch is 1.20 and declares Forge. Whether Alex's Mobs Continued satisfies the intended support remains open.
