# The cabin has an arboretum for growing trees

Status: Draft
Feature ID: B-0023

## Goal

Give the household a dedicated forestry room, called the arboretum, with space to grow trees.

## Stories and acceptance

### S1: Add the arboretum

Story: An owner purchases the arboretum and the household enters its dedicated tree-growing space.

Acceptance:

- The arboretum connects to the south side of the west corridor.
- Its starting usable floor is 7×7 with five blocks of clear height.
- Installation, traversal, and persistence follow B-0005.

### S2: Expand the arboretum

Story: An owner upgrades the arboretum to give the household more space for trees.

Acceptance:

- The usable floor grows to a maximum of 21×41.
- Clear height grows with floor size, reaching twenty blocks at maximum size.
- The room's reserved space accommodates its maximum floor and height without overlapping other rooms.

## Scope

The arboretum is the forestry room in B-0023. This draft records its placement and size limits. Manual tree-growing behavior still needs shaping.

## Non-goals

- Forestry automation, defined by B-0026.
- Adding another room for the arboretum name.
