# The cabin has a greenhouse

Status: Draft
Feature ID: B-0019

## Goal

Give the household dedicated space for planting and harvesting crops.

## Stories and acceptance

### S1: Add the greenhouse

Story: An owner purchases a greenhouse and the household enters its growing space.

Acceptance:

- The greenhouse branches from the south side of the west corridor, closest to the main room.
- Its starting usable floor is 5×7.
- Installation, traversal, permissions, and persistence follow B-0005.

### S2: Expand the greenhouse

Story: An owner upgrades the greenhouse to provide more growing space.

Acceptance:

- The usable floor grows to a maximum of 11×31 within its reserved space.
- Upgrade materials use glass and iron as base materials, with plants, seeds, and botanical finds as exploration requirements.

## Scope

This draft records the greenhouse's placement, size limits, and material direction. Manual crop behavior still needs shaping.

## Non-goals

Automatic harvesting and replanting belong to B-0026.

- Intermediate sizes, height, rectangular orientation, entrance placement, upgrade count, and costs remain open.
- Crop profiles, planting, growth, harvesting, and the effect of expansion on crops and player contents remain open.
