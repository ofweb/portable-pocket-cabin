# The stable can have an aquatic berth

Status: Draft
Feature ID: B-0021

## Goal

Extend the stable with water space for a tamed aquatic resident.

## Stories and acceptance

### S1: Add water space to the stable

Story: An owner adds an aquatic berth to the stable for a tamed aquatic animal.

Acceptance:

- The berth extends B-0020 rather than adding a separate general-purpose room.
- Its space must fit the stable's reserved layout without overlapping other rooms.

## Scope

This draft preserves the existing aquatic-berth feature and its relationship to the stable. Its behavior and dimensions still need shaping.

## Non-goals

Land-animal stalls and livestock rooms belong to their own features.

## Open questions and assumptions

- Inclusion in the first stable layout, floor dimensions, water depth, height, placement, and growth limits remain open.
- Animal profiles, occupancy, housing, release, safe water containment, upgrades, and costs remain open.
- The backlog proposes a tamed aquatic resident such as a nautilus; supported residents are not yet settled.
