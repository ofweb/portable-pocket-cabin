# Raw ingredient sources

This document says where every raw ingredient in the [intermediate products](intermediates.md) and [recipe catalogue](recipe-catalogue.md) comes from. Biomes use Minecraft's own names. Where a plant does not exist in Minecraft yet, the biome is where its wild form is found. Players can then grow most plants anywhere their growing needs are met, including in the cabin greenhouse. Growing needs belong to B-0050.

How players get each item:

- **Vanilla:** it already exists in Minecraft and works as it does today.
- **Farm:** grow it from seeds or cuttings found in the wild.
- **Tree:** grow a fruit-bearing tree from a sapling found in the wild, and harvest it.
- **Forage:** gather it from wild plants. Some can also be farmed.
- **Fish:** catch it with a rod or trap.
- **Raise:** keep the animal, then slaughter it for its carcass.
- **Mine:** dig it out of the ground.

## What this shows

- **Travel matters.** Jungles supply most spices and tropical fruit, savannas the warm crops and the Mediterranean ones, cherry groves East Asian ingredients, and taigas berries and cold crops. A player who wants to cook widely must explore widely, then bring cuttings and seeds home to the greenhouse.
- **One plant, several ingredients.** Cilantro gives coriander seed, olives are green or black by ripeness, the elder gives elderflowers in spring and elderberries in autumn, and tea becomes sencha, matcha, or black tea by how it is processed.
- **New world content is large.** About 125 raw ingredients are new: four kinds of animal carcass, six kinds of seafood, sea plants, rock salt, and over 100 plants. B-0049 and B-0050 own them, and their shaping should start from this list.

## Decisions

- **Seasons:** elderflower and elderberry, and rosehip, come from two growth stages of the same bush, because Minecraft has no seasons.
- **Biome fit:** windswept savannas stand in for a Mediterranean biome, for olives, rosemary, and oregano.
- **Plant count:** all 118 plants stay distinct, each with its own seeds and art.
- **Fruit trees:** fruit grows on fruiting leaves of new tree species and is picked without breaking the tree.
