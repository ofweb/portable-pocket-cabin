# Pad Thai

Thai · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [rice noodles](../products/rice-noodles.md), [tamarind paste](../products/tamarind-paste.md), [fish sauce](../products/fish-sauce.md), [palm sugar](../products/palm-sugar.md), [egg](../ingredients/egg.md), [tofu](../products/tofu.md) or [prawns](../ingredients/prawns.md), [bean sprouts](../ingredients/bean-sprouts.md), [roasted peanuts](../products/roasted-peanuts.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [soak](../techniques/soaking.md) noodles
2. [Wok](../stations/wok.md) (high): [stir-fry](../techniques/stir-frying.md) with sauce, egg, tofu, and sprouts
3. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): top with peanuts
