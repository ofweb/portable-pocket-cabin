Danish · Drinks. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): dissolve sugar

3. [Strainer](../stations/strainer.md): [strain](../techniques/straining.md) and bottle
