# Miso soup

Japanese · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [dashi](../products/dashi.md), [miso](../products/miso.md), [tofu](../products/tofu.md), [dried wakame](../products/dried-wakame.md), [spring onion](../ingredients/spring-onion.md)

1. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): [cube](../techniques/dicing.md) tofu, [slice](../techniques/slicing.md) spring onion
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): heat dashi, dissolve miso, add tofu and wakame
