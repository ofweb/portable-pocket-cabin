# Tub tim grob

Thai · Desserts and snacks. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [water chestnut](../ingredients/water-chestnut.md), [tapioca starch](../products/tapioca-starch.md), [beetroot](../ingredients/beetroot.md), [coconut milk](../products/coconut-milk.md), [simple syrup](../products/simple-syrup.md), [crushed ice](../products/crushed-ice.md)

1. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): [dice](../techniques/dicing.md) water chestnuts
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): colour with beetroot, coat in starch
3. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (high): [boil](../techniques/boiling.md)
4. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [serve](../techniques/assembling.md) in coconut milk syrup over ice
