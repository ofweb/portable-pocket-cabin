1. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): [dice](../techniques/dicing.md) vegetables
2. [Skillet](../stations/skillet.md) (medium): [sauté](../techniques/sauteing.md) each vegetable in olive oil
3. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [stew](../techniques/stewing.md) together with garlic and herbs
