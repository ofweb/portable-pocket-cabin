# Mango sticky rice

Thai · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [glutinous rice](../ingredients/glutinous-rice.md), [coconut milk](../products/coconut-milk.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md), [salt](../products/salt.md), [mango](../ingredients/mango.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (medium): [steam](../techniques/steaming.md) soaked rice
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): warm sweet coconut sauce
3. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [soak](../techniques/soaking.md) rice in sauce
4. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [slice](../techniques/slicing.md) mango, add salted coconut cream
