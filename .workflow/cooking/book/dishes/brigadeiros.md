# Brigadeiros

South American · Desserts and snacks. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [condensed milk](../products/condensed-milk.md), [cocoa powder](../products/cocoa-powder.md), [raw butter](../products/raw-butter.md), [chocolate](../products/chocolate.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): cook until thick
2. [Ageing shelf](../stations/ageing-shelf.md): [cool](../techniques/chilling.md)
3. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [roll](../techniques/rolling.md) balls, coat in grated chocolate
