# Pad kra pao

Thai · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [minced pork](../products/minced-pork.md), [bird's eye chili](../ingredients/bird-s-eye-chili.md), [garlic](../ingredients/garlic.md), [holy basil](../ingredients/holy-basil.md), [oyster sauce](../products/oyster-sauce.md), [fish sauce](../products/fish-sauce.md), [steamed rice](../products/steamed-rice.md), [egg](../ingredients/egg.md)

1. [Mortar and pestle](../stations/mortar-and-pestle.md): [pound](../techniques/pounding.md) chili and garlic
2. [Wok](../stations/wok.md) (high): [stir-fry](../techniques/stir-frying.md) pork with sauces and basil
3. [Skillet](../stations/skillet.md) (high): crisp-fry egg
4. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [serve](../techniques/assembling.md) over rice
