# Sauerbraten

German · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [beef round](../products/beef-round.md), [wine vinegar](../products/wine-vinegar.md), [red wine](../products/red-wine.md), [pickling spice](../products/pickling-spice.md), [onion](../ingredients/onion.md), [beef stock](../products/beef-stock.md), [gingerbread](../products/gingerbread.md), [raisins](../products/raisins.md)

1. [Brine barrel](../stations/brine-barrel.md): [marinate](../techniques/marinating.md) for three days
2. [Skillet](../stations/skillet.md) (high): [sear](../techniques/searing.md)
3. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [braise](../techniques/braising.md) in the marinade, thicken with gingerbread and raisins
