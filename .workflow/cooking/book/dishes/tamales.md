# Tamales

Mexican · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [masa](../products/masa.md), [lard](../products/lard.md), [chicken stock](../products/chicken-stock.md), [red chili sauce](../products/red-chili-sauce.md), [pork shoulder](../products/pork-shoulder.md), [dried corn husks](../products/dried-corn-husks.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): cook pork in chili sauce
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [beat](../techniques/beating.md) masa with lard and stock
3. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): spread on soaked husks, [fill](../techniques/filling.md), [wrap](../techniques/wrapping.md)
4. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (medium): [steam](../techniques/steaming.md)
