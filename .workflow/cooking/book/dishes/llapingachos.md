# Llapingachos

South American · Desserts and snacks. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [potato](../ingredients/potato.md), [queso fresco](../products/queso-fresco.md), [onion](../ingredients/onion.md), [achiote oil](../products/achiote-oil.md), [peanut sauce](../products/peanut-sauce.md), [fried egg](../products/fried-egg.md), [chorizo](../products/chorizo.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (high): [boil](../techniques/boiling.md) potatoes
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [mash](../techniques/mashing.md) with achiote oil
3. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): [stuff](../techniques/stuffing.md) with cheese, [shape](../techniques/shaping.md)
4. [Skillet](../stations/skillet.md) (medium): [fry](../techniques/frying.md)
5. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [serve](../techniques/assembling.md) with sauce, egg, and chorizo
