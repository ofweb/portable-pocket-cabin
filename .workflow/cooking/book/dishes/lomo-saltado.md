# Lomo saltado

South American · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [beef sirloin](../products/beef-sirloin.md), [red onion](../ingredients/red-onion.md), [tomato](../ingredients/tomato.md), [ají amarillo](../ingredients/aji-amarillo.md), [soy sauce](../products/soy-sauce.md), [wine vinegar](../products/wine-vinegar.md), [fried potatoes](../products/fried-potatoes.md), [steamed rice](../products/steamed-rice.md)

1. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): cut beef into strips
2. [Wok](../stations/wok.md) (high): [stir-fry](../techniques/stir-frying.md) beef, onion, and tomato with soy and vinegar, toss with fries
3. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [serve](../techniques/assembling.md) with rice
