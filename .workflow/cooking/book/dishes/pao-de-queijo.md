# Pão de queijo

South American · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [sour cassava starch](../products/sour-cassava-starch.md), [milk](../ingredients/milk.md), [vegetable oil](../products/vegetable-oil.md), [egg](../ingredients/egg.md), [Minas cheese](../products/minas-cheese.md), [salt](../products/salt.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [scald](../techniques/scalding.md) starch with hot milk and oil
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [mix](../techniques/mixing.md) in egg and cheese
3. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [roll](../techniques/rolling.md) balls
4. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (medium): [bake](../techniques/baking.md)
