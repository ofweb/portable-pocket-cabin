# Pastel de choclo

South American · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [pino](../products/pino.md), [hard-boiled egg](../products/hard-boiled-egg.md), [black olive](../ingredients/black-olive.md), [corn](../ingredients/corn.md), [basil](../ingredients/basil.md), [milk](../ingredients/milk.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md)

1. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [grind](../techniques/grinding.md) fresh corn
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): cook corn with basil and milk
3. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (high): [layer](../techniques/layering.md) pino, egg, olives, and corn, sprinkle sugar, [bake](../techniques/baking.md)
