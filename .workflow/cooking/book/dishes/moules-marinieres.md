# Moules marinières

French · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [mussels](../ingredients/mussels.md), [shallot](../ingredients/shallot.md), [garlic](../ingredients/garlic.md), [white wine](../products/white-wine.md), [salted butter](../products/salted-butter.md), [parsley](../ingredients/parsley.md)

1. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): [chop](../techniques/chopping.md) shallot and parsley
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (medium): sweat aromatics in butter, add wine and mussels, [steam](../techniques/steaming.md)
