# Flæskesteg med rødkål

Danish · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [pork loin with rind](../products/pork-loin-with-rind.md), [coarse salt](../products/coarse-salt.md), [bay leaf](../products/bay-leaf.md), [red cabbage](../ingredients/red-cabbage.md), [wine vinegar](../products/wine-vinegar.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md), [salted butter](../products/salted-butter.md), [potato](../ingredients/potato.md), [pork stock](../products/pork-stock.md)

1. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): score the rind
2. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (high): [roast](../techniques/roasting.md) until the rind crackles
3. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [braise](../techniques/braising.md) red cabbage with vinegar and sugar
4. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): make gravy from the drippings
