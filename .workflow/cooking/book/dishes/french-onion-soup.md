# French onion soup

French · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [onion](../ingredients/onion.md), [salted butter](../products/salted-butter.md), [beef stock](../products/beef-stock.md), [bread](../products/bread.md), [aged hard cheese](../products/aged-hard-cheese.md)

1. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): [slice](../techniques/slicing.md) onions
2. [Skillet](../stations/skillet.md) (low): [caramelise](../techniques/caramelising.md) in butter
3. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [simmer](../techniques/simmering.md) with stock
4. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (high): top with bread and cheese, [gratinate](../techniques/gratinating.md)
