# Crème brûlée

French · Desserts and snacks. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [cream](../products/cream.md), [egg](../ingredients/egg.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md), [vanilla](../products/vanilla.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [infuse](../techniques/infusing.md) cream with vanilla
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [temper](../techniques/tempering.md) yolks
3. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (low): [bake](../techniques/baking.md) in a water bath
4. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (high): [caramelise](../techniques/caramelising.md) the sugar top
