# Feijoada

South American · Main dishes. Defined in the [recipe catalogue](../../recipe-catalogue.md).

## Recipe

Ingredients: [dried black beans](../products/dried-black-beans.md), [carne seca](../products/carne-seca.md), [smoked sausage](../products/smoked-sausage.md), [pork belly](../products/pork-belly.md), [pig trotters](../products/pig-trotters.md), [bay leaf](../products/bay-leaf.md), [garlic](../ingredients/garlic.md), [onion](../ingredients/onion.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [soak](../techniques/soaking.md) beans and desalt the beef
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [simmer](../techniques/simmering.md) with all meats for a long time
3. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [serve](../techniques/assembling.md) with rice, farofa, greens, and orange
