# Curdling

Setting milk into curds and whey with rennet, acid, or nigari. It is the first step of cheese and tofu.

Heat in a pot. Defined in the [techniques](../../techniques.md).

## Used in (3)

- [queso blanco](../products/queso-blanco.md)
- [queso fresco](../products/queso-fresco.md)
- [tofu](../products/tofu.md)
