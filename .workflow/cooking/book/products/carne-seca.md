# carne seca

Sausages and cured meat and fish. Feature: B-0048 for curing and smoking, using cuts and casings from B-0049. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [beef round](../products/beef-round.md), [coarse salt](../products/coarse-salt.md)

1. [Brine barrel](../stations/brine-barrel.md): salt
2. [Drying rack](../stations/drying-rack.md): [dry](../techniques/drying.md) in the sun
