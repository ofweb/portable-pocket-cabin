# katsuobushi

Sausages and cured meat and fish. Feature: B-0048 for curing and smoking, using cuts and casings from B-0049. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [skipjack tuna](../ingredients/skipjack-tuna.md), [salt](../products/salt.md)

1. [Butcher block](../stations/butcher-block.md): [fillet](../techniques/filleting.md) into loins
2. [Brine barrel](../stations/brine-barrel.md): salt
3. [Smoker](../stations/smoker.md): [smoke](../techniques/smoking.md)
4. [Drying rack](../stations/drying-rack.md): [dry](../techniques/drying.md) for a long time
