# pickled herring

Sausages and cured meat and fish. Feature: B-0048 for curing and smoking, using cuts and casings from B-0049. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

Ingredients: [herring](../ingredients/herring.md), [salt](../products/salt.md), [wine vinegar](../products/wine-vinegar.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md), [onion](../ingredients/onion.md), [pickling spice](../products/pickling-spice.md)

2. [Fermentation crock](../stations/fermentation-crock.md): [pickle](../techniques/pickling.md)
