# roasted coffee

Chocolate, tea, coffee, and drink bases. Feature: B-0052 for drinks, with B-0050 for the plants. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [coffee cherry](../ingredients/coffee-cherry.md)

1. [Fermentation crock](../stations/fermentation-crock.md): [ferment](../techniques/fermenting.md)
2. [Drying rack](../stations/drying-rack.md): [dry](../techniques/drying.md)
3. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): hull
4. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (high): [roast](../techniques/roasting.md)
5. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [grind](../techniques/grinding.md)
