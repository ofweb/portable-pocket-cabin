# anko

Sauces, pastes, and condiments. Feature: B-0047. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [dried azuki beans](../products/dried-azuki-beans.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [simmer](../techniques/simmering.md) until soft
2. [Mortar and pestle](../stations/mortar-and-pestle.md): [mash](../techniques/mashing.md)
3. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): cook with sugar until thick

## Used in

- [Daifuku mochi](../dishes/daifuku-mochi.md) (dish)
- [Dorayaki](../dishes/dorayaki.md) (dish)
- [Taiyaki](../dishes/taiyaki.md) (dish)
