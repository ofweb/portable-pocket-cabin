# duck confit

Sausages and cured meat and fish. Feature: B-0048 for curing and smoking, using cuts and casings from B-0049. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [duck legs](../products/duck-legs.md), [coarse salt](../products/coarse-salt.md), [garlic](../ingredients/garlic.md), [thyme](../ingredients/thyme.md)

1. [Brine barrel](../stations/brine-barrel.md): salt overnight
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): cook slowly in its own fat
