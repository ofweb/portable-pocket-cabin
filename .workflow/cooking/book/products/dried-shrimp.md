# dried shrimp

Sausages and cured meat and fish. Feature: B-0048 for curing and smoking, using cuts and casings from B-0049. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [prawns](../ingredients/prawns.md), [salt](../products/salt.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (high): [boil](../techniques/boiling.md) in salted water
2. [Drying rack](../stations/drying-rack.md): [dry](../techniques/drying.md)
