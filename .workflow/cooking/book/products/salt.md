# salt

Salt and sugars. Feature: B-0047, which owns salt as a proposal. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

### Way 1

Ingredients: [water bottle of seawater](../ingredients/water-bottle-of-seawater.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (high): [boil](../techniques/boiling.md) down
2. [Drying rack](../stations/drying-rack.md): [dry](../techniques/drying.md) the crystals

### Way 2

Ingredients: [rock salt](../ingredients/rock-salt.md)

1. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [grind](../techniques/grinding.md)
