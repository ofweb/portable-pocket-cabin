# sourdough starter

Leavening, doughs, and pastry. Feature: B-0046. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [wheat flour](../products/wheat-flour.md) or [rye flour](../products/rye-flour.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md)

1. [Fermentation crock](../stations/fermentation-crock.md): start a [culture](../techniques/culturing.md) for a day. Feeding one starter with flour and water gives two.
