# Mexican chocolate

Chocolate, tea, coffee, and drink bases. Feature: B-0052 for drinks, with B-0050 for the plants. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [roasted cocoa nibs](../products/roasted-cocoa-nibs.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md), [cinnamon](../products/cinnamon.md)

1. [Mortar and pestle](../stations/mortar-and-pestle.md): [grind](../techniques/grinding.md) coarsely
2. [Ageing shelf](../stations/ageing-shelf.md): [set](../techniques/setting.md) in discs

## Used in

- [Champurrado](../dishes/champurrado.md) (dish)
- [mole paste](../products/mole-paste.md)
