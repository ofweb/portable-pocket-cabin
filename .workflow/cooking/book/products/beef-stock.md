# beef stock

Stocks and broths. Feature: B-0047. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [beef bones](../products/beef-bones.md), [onion](../ingredients/onion.md), [carrot](../ingredients/carrot.md), [celery](../ingredients/celery.md), [bay leaf](../products/bay-leaf.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md)

1. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (high): [roast](../techniques/roasting.md) bones
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [simmer](../techniques/simmering.md) for a long time
3. [Strainer](../stations/strainer.md): [strain](../techniques/straining.md)
