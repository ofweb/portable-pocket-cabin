# yerba mate

Chocolate, tea, coffee, and drink bases. Feature: B-0052 for drinks, with B-0050 for the plants. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [yerba mate leaf](../ingredients/yerba-mate-leaf.md)

1. [Skillet](../stations/skillet.md) (high): [sear](../techniques/searing.md)
2. [Drying rack](../stations/drying-rack.md): [dry](../techniques/drying.md)
3. [Ageing shelf](../stations/ageing-shelf.md): [age](../techniques/ageing.md) for months
4. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [crush](../techniques/crushing.md)

## Used in
