# aged hard cheese

Dairy and eggs. Feature: B-0046. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [curds](../products/curds.md), [salt](../products/salt.md)

1. [Cheese press](../stations/cheese-press.md): [press](../techniques/pressing.md) hard
2. [Brine barrel](../stations/brine-barrel.md): [brine](../techniques/brining.md)
3. [Ageing shelf](../stations/ageing-shelf.md): [age](../techniques/ageing.md) for weeks
