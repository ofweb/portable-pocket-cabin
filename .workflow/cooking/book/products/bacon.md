# bacon

Sausages and cured meat and fish. Feature: B-0048 for curing and smoking, using cuts and casings from B-0049. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [pork belly](../products/pork-belly.md), [salt](../products/salt.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md)

1. [Brine barrel](../stations/brine-barrel.md): [dry](../techniques/drying.md)-cure for a week
2. [Smoker](../stations/smoker.md): [smoke](../techniques/smoking.md)
