# chocolate

Chocolate, tea, coffee, and drink bases. Feature: B-0052 for drinks, with B-0050 for the plants. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [roasted cocoa nibs](../products/roasted-cocoa-nibs.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md), [cocoa butter](../products/cocoa-butter.md)

1. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [grind](../techniques/grinding.md) for a long time
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [temper](../techniques/tempering.md)
3. [Ageing shelf](../stations/ageing-shelf.md): [set](../techniques/setting.md)
