# hominy

Grains, flours, and starches. Feature: B-0046. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [dried corn](../products/dried-corn.md), [lye solution](../products/lye-solution.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [nixtamalize](../techniques/nixtamalizing.md), [simmer](../techniques/simmering.md) with lye
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): rinse and hull
