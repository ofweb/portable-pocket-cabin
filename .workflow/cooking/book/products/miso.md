# miso

Fermented, pickled, and dried. Feature: B-0048. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [dried soybeans](../products/dried-soybeans.md), [koji rice](../products/koji-rice.md), [salt](../products/salt.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): cook soybeans
2. [Mortar and pestle](../stations/mortar-and-pestle.md): [mash](../techniques/mashing.md)
3. [Fermentation crock](../stations/fermentation-crock.md): [age](../techniques/ageing.md) for five days

## Used in

- [Miso soup](../dishes/miso-soup.md) (dish)
