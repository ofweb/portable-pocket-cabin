# peanut sauce

Sauces, pastes, and condiments. Feature: B-0047. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [roasted peanuts](../products/roasted-peanuts.md), [coconut milk](../products/coconut-milk.md), [red curry paste](../products/red-curry-paste.md), [palm sugar](../products/palm-sugar.md), [tamarind paste](../products/tamarind-paste.md)

1. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [grind](../techniques/grinding.md) peanuts
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [simmer](../techniques/simmering.md) together
