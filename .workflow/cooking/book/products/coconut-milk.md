# coconut milk

## How it is made

Ingredients: [coconut](../ingredients/coconut.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md)

1. [Cutting board](../stations/cutting-board.md): [crack](../techniques/cracking.md) and [grate](../techniques/grating.md) the flesh
2. [Press](../stations/press.md): [press](../techniques/pressing.md) with warm water
