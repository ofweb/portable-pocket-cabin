# soy sauce

Fermented, pickled, and dried. Feature: B-0048. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [dried soybeans](../products/dried-soybeans.md), [wheat flour](../products/wheat-flour.md), [koji spores](../products/koji-spores.md), [salt](../products/salt.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (medium): [steam](../techniques/steaming.md) soybeans
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [mix](../techniques/mixing.md) with roasted wheat and spores
3. [Fermentation crock](../stations/fermentation-crock.md): [ferment](../techniques/fermenting.md) in [brine](../techniques/brining.md) for weeks
4. [Press](../stations/press.md): [press](../techniques/pressing.md)
