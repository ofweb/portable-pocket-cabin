# panela

Salt and sugars. Feature: B-0047, which owns salt as a proposal. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [sugar cane](../ingredients/sugar-cane.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md)

1. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [crush](../techniques/crushing.md) cane
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (high): [boil](../techniques/boiling.md) the juice down
3. [Ageing shelf](../stations/ageing-shelf.md): [set](../techniques/setting.md) in blocks
