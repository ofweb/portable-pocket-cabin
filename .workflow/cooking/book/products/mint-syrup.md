# mint syrup

Chocolate, tea, coffee, and drink bases. Feature: B-0052 for drinks, with B-0050 for the plants. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [mint](../ingredients/mint.md), [sugar](../ingredients/sugar.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): dissolve sugar
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [steep](../techniques/steeping.md) mint
3. [Strainer](../stations/strainer.md): [strain](../techniques/straining.md)
