# ajitsuke tamago

Dairy and eggs. Feature: B-0046. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [egg](../ingredients/egg.md), [soy sauce](../products/soy-sauce.md), [mirin](../products/mirin.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (high): [boil](../techniques/boiling.md) soft
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [peel](../techniques/peeling.md) and [marinate](../techniques/marinating.md) overnight
