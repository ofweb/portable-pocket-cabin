# curds

Dairy and eggs. Feature: B-0046. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [milk](../ingredients/milk.md), [rennet](../products/rennet.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): warm milk, add rennet, cut the [set](../techniques/setting.md) curd. Also gives [whey](../products/whey.md).
