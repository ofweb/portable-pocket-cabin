# puff pastry

## How it is made

Ingredients: [wheat flour](../products/wheat-flour.md), [raw butter](../products/raw-butter.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md), [salt](../products/salt.md)

1. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): make dough
2. [Prep counter](../stations/prep-counter.md): [fold](../techniques/folding.md) butter in, [roll](../techniques/rolling.md), and fold six times
3. [Ageing shelf](../stations/ageing-shelf.md): [chill](../techniques/chilling.md)
