# mole paste

Sauces, pastes, and condiments. Feature: B-0047. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [dried chilies](../products/dried-chilies.md), [roasted peanuts](../products/roasted-peanuts.md), [toasted sesame seeds](../products/toasted-sesame-seeds.md), [Mexican chocolate](../products/mexican-chocolate.md), [raisins](../products/raisins.md), [cinnamon](../products/cinnamon.md), [cloves](../products/cloves.md), [corn tortillas](../products/corn-tortillas.md)

1. [Skillet](../stations/skillet.md) (medium): [toast](../techniques/toasting.md) each ingredient
2. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [grind](../techniques/grinding.md) into a paste
