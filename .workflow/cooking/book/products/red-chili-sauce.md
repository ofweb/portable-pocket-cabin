# red chili sauce

Sauces, pastes, and condiments. Feature: B-0047. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [dried chilies](../products/dried-chilies.md), [garlic](../ingredients/garlic.md), [cumin](../products/cumin.md), [pork stock](../products/pork-stock.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [soak](../techniques/soaking.md) chilies
2. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [blend](../techniques/blending.md)
3. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [fry](../techniques/frying.md) and [simmer](../techniques/simmering.md)
