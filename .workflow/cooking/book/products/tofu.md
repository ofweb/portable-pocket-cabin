# tofu

Fermented, pickled, and dried. Feature: B-0048. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [dried soybeans](../products/dried-soybeans.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md), [nigari](../products/nigari.md)

1. [Mill](../stations/mill.md): [grind](../techniques/grinding.md) soaked beans
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): cook
3. [Strainer](../stations/strainer.md): [strain](../techniques/straining.md) into soy milk
4. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [curdle](../techniques/curdling.md) with nigari
5. [Cheese press](../stations/cheese-press.md): [press](../techniques/pressing.md)

## Used in

- [Miso soup](../dishes/miso-soup.md) (dish)
- [Pad Thai](../dishes/pad-thai.md) (dish)
