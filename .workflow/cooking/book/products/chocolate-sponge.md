1. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [whisk](../techniques/whisking.md) eggs and sugar, [fold](../techniques/folding.md) in flour and cocoa
2. [Oven](../stations/oven.md) (medium): [bake](../techniques/baking.md)
