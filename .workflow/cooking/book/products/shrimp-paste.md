# shrimp paste

## How it is made

Ingredients: [prawns](../ingredients/prawns.md), [salt](../products/salt.md)

1. [Mortar and pestle](../stations/mortar-and-pestle.md): [pound](../techniques/pounding.md)
2. [Fermentation crock](../stations/fermentation-crock.md): [ferment](../techniques/fermenting.md) for weeks
3. [Drying rack](../stations/drying-rack.md): [dry](../techniques/drying.md)
