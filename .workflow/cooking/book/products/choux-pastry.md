# choux pastry

Leavening, doughs, and pastry. Feature: B-0046. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [wheat flour](../products/wheat-flour.md), [raw butter](../products/raw-butter.md), [water](../ingredients/water.md), [egg](../ingredients/egg.md)

1. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): cook flour into the butter and water
2. [Mixing bowl](../stations/mixing-bowl.md): [beat](../techniques/beating.md) in eggs

- [Profiteroles](../dishes/profiteroles.md) (dish)
