# cocktail sausages

Sausages and cured meat and fish. Feature: B-0048 for curing and smoking, using cuts and casings from B-0049. Defined in the [intermediate products](../../intermediates.md).

## How it is made

Ingredients: [minced pork](../products/minced-pork.md), [salt](../products/salt.md), [pork casing](../products/pork-casing.md)

1. [Sausage stuffer](../stations/sausage-stuffer.md): [stuff](../techniques/stuffing.md) thin links
2. [Cooking pot](../stations/cooking-pot.md) (low): [poach](../techniques/poaching.md)
3. [Smoker](../stations/smoker.md): [smoke](../techniques/smoking.md)
