# Oven

Cooking with heat. Defined in the [station list](../../stations.md).

- **Kind:** Container with its own fuel
- **Heat:** Low 11, medium 27, high 14
- **Supports:** Bake, roast, gratinate, dry, burn logs to ash, bake in moulds
- **Feature:** B-0045
