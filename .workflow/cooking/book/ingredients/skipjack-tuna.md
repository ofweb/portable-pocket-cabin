# skipjack tuna

Fish and seafood. Defined in the [sources](../../sources.md).

Skipjack tuna is the fish katsuobushi is made from. Japanese cooking calls it katsuo, and English often calls it bonito, which is why its shaved flakes are sold as bonito flakes.

## Where it comes from

- **Where:** Warm oceans
- **How:** Fish
- **Feature:** B-0049

## Used in

- [katsuobushi](../products/katsuobushi.md)
