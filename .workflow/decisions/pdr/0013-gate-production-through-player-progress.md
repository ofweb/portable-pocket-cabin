# PDR-0013: Production follows player progress

Status: Accepted

## Context

A resource can be hard to get at first and easy to get after player progress. Cabin production should replace routine gathering. Upgrade costs must express that progression while allowing players to help each other.

## Decision

Production at higher tiers uses advanced upgrade materials as its progression gate. Required materials and manufactured products belong to a stage of Survival mode where gathering the automated resource should be routine. Balance must consider the ingredients and work behind manufactured products, not just their item counts.

Previously collected and traded materials satisfy the gate. There is no separate personal achievement, biome-visit, or resource-gathering requirement for the owner. A more advanced player can help another household unlock automation.

Coal production follows this rule. Its upgrade materials should place it after the stage where obtaining coal presents a meaningful challenge. Once installed, production replaces the routine gathering work.

## Rationale

Material requirements connect automation to Survival progression without measuring a player's skill or exposure to danger. Trade and household cooperation remain useful. This is an approximation of readiness, not proof that each owner has personally completed the resource work.

## Scope

This decision applies to resource production at higher tiers. It does not select tiers, exact costs, recipes, or controls. Shape must identify the stage where gathering each capability's resource should be routine. Balance work must keep its material gate consistent with that stage. Cabin books and other capability prerequisites still apply.
