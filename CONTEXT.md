# Portable Pocket Cabin Domain

Portable Pocket Cabin models one persistent travelling home whose protected spaces, household and upgrades remain bound to the cabin rather than to any exterior projection or player inventory.

## Upgrade language

**Tracked upgrade**:
The single eligible cabin upgrade selected by its owner to receive material contributions.
_Avoid_: Active recipe, selected storage

**Upgrade fund**:
Cabin-owned materials committed exclusively to the tracked upgrade and limited by that upgrade's requirements.
_Avoid_: Upgrade storage, upgrade chest, material inventory

**Upgrade contribution**:
Required material deliberately transferred by an authorised household member into the upgrade fund.
_Avoid_: Inventory payment, automatic collection

**Upgrade installation**:
The owner's deliberate commitment of a fully funded upgrade, consuming its fund and applying its purchased effect.
_Avoid_: Automatic purchase, crafting the upgrade

**Purchased upgrade**:
An upgrade effect that the owner has installed and the cabin now owns, distinct from an upgrade that is merely tracked or funded.
_Avoid_: Tracked upgrade, funded upgrade

**Upgrade refund receipt**:
The exact materials paid for one reversible purchased-upgrade step, retained as the basis for a later owner-authorised refund.
_Avoid_: Current recipe, refund value

## Window language

**Cabin window**:
An independently purchased functional opening on an eligible cabin wall that signals conditions outside the deployed cabin.
_Avoid_: Real portal, cosmetic glass

**Window tier**:
The purchased size stage of one cabin window, independent of every other window's tier.
_Avoid_: Cabin window level, wall tier
