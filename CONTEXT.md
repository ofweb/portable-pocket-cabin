# Portable Pocket Cabin Domain

Portable Pocket Cabin models one persistent travelling home whose protected spaces, household and upgrades remain bound to the cabin rather than to any exterior projection or player inventory.

## Upgrade language

**Upgrade fund**:
Cabin-owned materials committed to one stable upgrade target and capped by its exact requirements.
A cabin may have several upgrade funds but no unassigned material wallet.
_Avoid_: Tracked upgrade, general storage, upgrade chest

**Upgrade contribution**:
Required material deliberately transferred into one upgrade fund.
_Avoid_: Inventory payment, automatic collection

**Upgrade installation**:
The owner's deliberate commitment of a fully funded upgrade, consuming its fund and applying the purchased effect.
_Avoid_: Automatic purchase, crafting the upgrade

**Purchased upgrade**:
An installed upgrade effect owned by the cabin, distinct from a merely funded upgrade.
_Avoid_: Funded upgrade

**Upgrade refund receipt**:
The exact materials paid for one reversible upgrade step and retained for a later owner-authorised refund.
_Avoid_: Current recipe, refund value

## Window language

**Cabin window**:
A purchased, tiered window on an eligible cabin wall that represents conditions outside the deployed cabin.
_Avoid_: Real portal, cosmetic glass

**Window tier**:
The purchased size stage of one cabin window. Each window has its own tier.
_Avoid_: Cabin window level, wall tier
