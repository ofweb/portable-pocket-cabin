# Portable Pocket Cabin

Portable Pocket Cabin is a travelling-home domain in which one persistent pocket home can move its exterior entrance between campsites without carrying the surrounding settlement with it.

## Cabin magic

**World attunement**:
The permanent material pattern chosen once for a world save that resolves variable cabin-upgrade requirements for every player in that world.
_Avoid_: Per-player recipes, dimension recipes

**Resonance**:
The active cabin magic carried and shaped by amethyst; it preserves patterns and performs enchanting, automation and other magical work.
_Avoid_: Power, mana

**Dimensional Logic Core**:
The cabin component that defines and controls the rules of one pocket space through copper, redstone and amethyst resonance.
_Avoid_: Computer, processor

**Dimensional Anchor**:
The cabin component that gives a pocket space a stable identity and location so its contents persist when no exterior is deployed.
_Avoid_: Fuel, power source

**Dimensional Folding Core**:
The cabin component that forms a controlled connection between an exterior entrance and its anchored pocket space.
_Avoid_: Portal core, teleporter

**Dimensional Foundation**:
The assembled dimensional machinery that combines a Logic Core, Dimensional Anchor and Folding Core into the base of a cabin.
_Avoid_: Machine block

**Cabin Kit**:
An unbound portable cabin containing a Dimensional Foundation whose first successful deployment creates and binds its permanent cabin identity.
_Avoid_: Portal pocket cabin, cabin item

**Cabin palette**:
The authoritative selection of floor, wall, roof/ceiling and door materials captured when a Cabin Kit is crafted, applied to both its exterior and pocket interior, and preserved across packing, restart and redeployment.
_Avoid_: Cosmetic variant, exterior skin

## Cabin animals

**Stable resident**:
A specific tamed rideable animal checked into a cabin stable while retaining its individual identity.
_Avoid_: Stored mob, livestock

**Aquatic berth**:
A flooded upgraded stable stall that safely houses an eligible aquatic stable resident such as a nautilus.
_Avoid_: Aquarium

**House cat**:
A tamed cat that has been given one cabin as its home and lives there as a companion rather than as a stable resident or livestock population.
_Avoid_: Cat storage, cat production
